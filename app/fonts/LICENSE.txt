inter-latin-var.woff2 is the latin-subset variable font (weights 100-900),
fetched from Google Fonts (Inter v20) and vendored locally so `next build`
never depends on fonts.googleapis.com being reachable — fresh installs on
restricted networks previously hard-failed the Turbopack build on that fetch
(drill:fresh-windows, 2026-08-06).
